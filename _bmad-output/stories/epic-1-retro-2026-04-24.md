## Key Insights

1. **Trust no one, even deps.** We own repo, not dependencies. Each layer validates its own concerns explicitly.
2. **Deferrals must expire.** "Pre-existing pattern" = not valid reason. Fix at source or schedule cleanup.
3. **Tiger Style + TDD compound positively.** Strengthen in Epic 2.

---

## Team Agreements (effective Epic 2)

- Each layer (router, service, adapter) owns explicit validation. No delegating trust up, down, or to libs.
- `assert` = programmer invariant. `ValueError`/`HTTPException` = expected runtime failure. Never conflate.
- Story status updated to `done` before next story starts. No gaps.
